BusMix pan modal drag stability follow-up
Date: 2026-05-25

Problem
- User validation found the custom pan slider could jump through multiple positions while dragging, making the modal unusable.

Cause
- The first custom slider implementation used nativeEvent.locationX during move events.
- During a PanResponder gesture, locationX may become unstable when the finger crosses nested visual layers such as fill, center marker, or thumb.

Fix
- PanControlModal now captures the initial pan value on gesture grant.
- Move updates derive from PanResponderGestureState.dx and measured track width.
- Decorative slider layers now use pointerEvents="none" so they do not participate in touch targeting.
