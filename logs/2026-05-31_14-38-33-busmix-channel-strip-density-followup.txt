BusMix channel strip density follow-up
Date: 2026-05-31

Scope
- Follow-up to `fader-meter-single-rail`.
- Goal: slightly reduce each BusMix channel strip width so about 5.5 channels are visible on wider portrait phones before horizontal scroll is needed.

Changes
- Reduced `ChannelStrip` width from `86` to `71`.
- Updated `BusMixScreen.CHANNEL_STRIP_WIDTH` from `86` to `71`, keeping `CHANNEL_ITEM_LENGTH = CHANNEL_STRIP_WIDTH + CHANNEL_STRIP_GAP`.
- Effective item length is now `72` with the existing `1` px gap.
- Added single-line/font-shrink protection to the channel dB label so narrower strips do not wrap the value.

Preserved
- Fader rail/meter composition from `fader-meter-single-rail`.
- Fader cap size and gesture ownership.
- Meter subscription lifecycle.
- Fader math and OSC behavior.
- BusMix hooks, services, meter decoder/routing, presets, pan, mute/on, and remote-fader rollback state.

Verification
- `yarn tsc`: passed.
- `yarn jest __tests__/features/busMix --runInBand`: passed, 7 suites / 37 tests.
- `yarn jest __tests__/features/busGroups --runInBand`: passed, 2 suites / 10 tests.

Pending
- Manual device/simulator visual validation to confirm the exact 5.5-channel feel on the target phone width.
